Father's Day · خوشیوں کا دن

Happy Father’s
Day, Papa

Some people get superheroes from comic books. I got mine at home. Thank you for every quiet sacrifice, every lesson taught without words, and every time you made hard things look easy just so I wouldn’t have to worry. Today is for you.

For You, Papa

آپ سائے کی طرح ساتھ رہے ہیں، جب بھی دھوپ تیز ہوئی۔ آپ کی محنت، آپ کی دعائیں، میری ہر کامیابی میں شامل ہیں۔ یوم آباء مبارک ہو، بابا۔

You have stayed beside me like shade, whenever the sun grew harsh. Your hard work, your prayers, are woven into every success of mine. Happy Father’s Day, Baba.

With all my love Abdul Wahab
